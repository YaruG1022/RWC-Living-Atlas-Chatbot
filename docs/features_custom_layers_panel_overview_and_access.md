# Custom Layers Panel

## What Is the Custom Layers Panel?

The Custom Layers Panel is your personal library of saved ArcGIS map services. Once you save a service, it appears here so you can quickly add it to the map again without searching for it from scratch.

You must be **logged in** to use the Custom Layers Panel.

---

## How to Open the Custom Layers Panel

1. On the Home page, look at the left sidebar.
2. Click the **Custom Layers icon** (folder or layers icon).
3. The Custom Layers Panel opens on the left side of the screen.

**Note:** The Custom Layers panel and the ArcGIS Services panel cannot both be open at the same time. Opening one closes the other.

---

## How to Add a Saved Layer to the Map

1. Open the Custom Layers Panel.
2. Browse or search for the service you want.
3. Check the **checkbox** next to the service name to add all its layers to the map.
4. To add individual layers only, expand the service and check specific layers.

---

## How to Remove a Layer from the Map

1. Open the Custom Layers Panel.
2. Uncheck the checkbox next to the layer or service you added.
3. The layer is removed from the map (but remains in your saved library).

---

## How to Search Your Saved Layers

1. Open the Custom Layers Panel.
2. Type in the **search box** at the top of the panel.
3. Select the search type from the dropdown: Any, Folder, Service, or Layer.
4. Press Enter or click Search.
5. Matching results are highlighted and expanded automatically.
6. Use the **Previous / Next** arrows to navigate between matches.
7. Click **Clear** to reset the search.

---

## How to Create a Folder

1. Open the Custom Layers Panel.
2. Click the **New Folder** button in the panel header.
3. Type a folder name and confirm.
4. The new folder appears in your library.

---

## How to Rename a Folder

1. Right-click (or use the "..." menu) on the folder name.
2. Click **Rename**.
3. Type the new name and confirm.

---

## How to Delete a Folder

1. Right-click (or use the "..." menu) on the folder name.
2. Click **Delete Folder**.
3. Confirm the deletion. Services inside the folder are moved to Root.

---

## How to Move a Service to a Different Folder

1. Drag the service row and drop it onto the target folder.
2. The service is moved to that folder.

---

## How to Reorder Services

1. Drag a service row up or down within the same folder.
2. Release to set the new order.

---

## How to Remove a Service from Your Library

1. Right-click (or use the "..." menu) on the service.
2. Click **Remove from Custom Layers**.
3. The service is deleted from your library and its map layers are removed.

---

## How to Change Layer Opacity

1. Open the Custom Layers Panel.
2. Use the **opacity slider** at the top of the panel to control the global opacity of all custom layers.
3. Or use the opacity slider inside a service's info modal for per-service opacity.

---

## How to View Service Details

1. Right-click a service (or use the "..." menu).
2. Click **Learn More**.
3. An info modal opens showing the service description, layer tree, and a link to the external ArcGIS service page.

---

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I add a custom layer to the map?**
A: Open the Custom Layers Panel and check the checkbox next to the service or layer you want to add.

**Q: How do I save a service to my custom layers?**
A: Save it from the ArcGIS Services (GIS Services) panel — after finding a service there, use the save/bookmark action to add it to your Custom Layers library.

**Q: My custom layers panel shows a login message. Why?**
A: You must be logged in to use the Custom Layers Panel. Click Login in the navbar to sign in.

**Q: How do I delete a service from my saved library?**
A: Right-click the service in the Custom Layers Panel and choose "Remove from Custom Layers".

**Q: Can I organize my saved layers into folders?**
A: Yes. Click New Folder in the panel header to create folders, then drag services into them.

**Q: How do I show only layers currently added to the map?**
A: Use the "Show only services added to map" filter toggle at the top of the Custom Layers Panel.
