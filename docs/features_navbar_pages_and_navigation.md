# Navigation Bar (Navbar)

## What Is the Navbar?

The navigation bar at the top of every page gives you quick access to all major sections of the app.

---

## Navbar Links (Always Visible)

| Link | Page | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| **RWC Living Atlas** | Home | Click the app name to go to the main map workspace |
| **CEREO** | External site | Opens the CEREO website in a new tab |
| **Home** | Home | Main interactive map with all panels |
| **About** | About | Project mission and overview |
| **Contact** | Contact | Contact information (address, phone, email) |
| **Updates** | Update History | List of app releases and feature changes by date |
| **User Manual** | User Manual | Full in-app documentation for all features |

---

## When You Are Logged Out

The navbar shows:
- **Register** — submit a request for a new account
- **Login** — sign in to your account

---

## When You Are Logged In

The navbar shows:
- Your **username** and **avatar icon** in the top-right corner
- Click your avatar to open the user menu (Profile, Administration, Switch Account, Logout)

---

## Common Questions

**Q: How do I get to the main map?**
A: Click "Home" or the "RWC Living Atlas" title in the navigation bar.

**Q: Where is the login button?**
A: In the top-right corner of the navigation bar when you are not logged in. Click "Login".

**Q: Where is the User Manual?**
A: Click "User Manual" in the navigation bar.

**Q: How do I see what's new in the app?**
A: Click "Updates" in the navigation bar to see the release history.

**Q: How do I contact support or find contact information?**
A: Click "Contact" in the navigation bar.
